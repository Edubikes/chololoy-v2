Shrimp in Creamy Beer Sauce

Garlic shrimp in a lager and sour cream sauce. Two tablespoons of seasoning flavor the whole dish.

Garlic shrimp in a lager and sour cream sauce. Two tablespoons of seasoning flavor the whole dish.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons Chololoy® Original Seasoning
  • 8 oz peeled and deveined shrimp
  • ¼ cup sour cream
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • ½ can lager beer
  • Oil

Preparation

  1. In a nonstick skillet, heat the oil and garlic over medium heat. Add the shrimp seasoned with 1 tablespoon of Chololoy® Original Seasoning and cook through, then take out the garlic.
  2. In another skillet, warm the beer over medium-low heat and stir in the rest of the seasoning.
  3. Add the sour cream and let it warm up without boiling. Pour over the shrimp.

• Chololoy® is made only with natural ingredients and no preservatives.
