FAQ

Before you order

01What comes in a case?

Each case holds twelve 1-lb heat-sealed aluminum bags of Chololoy® Original Seasoning. Minimum order is 5 cases.

02How long does wholesale delivery take?

Wholesale orders take 4 to 6 weeks to deliver.

03Can you make a custom blend?

For large accounts, yes — from small adjustments, like the size of the onion pieces, to an exclusive blend based on the original. Kristina will work out the details with you directly.

04What is its shelf life?

It keeps for one year. It’s 100% natural with no preservatives.

05Can I buy it for home use?

Smaller sizes for home use are available on request. It also comes in a retail jar, for sale in retail stores. Contact Kristina. For restaurant quantities, order a wholesale case.
